### 7.4 Cross-field invariants

Enforce all of these:

1. A pending gate requires `decisionStatus: NOT_GRANTED` and `decision: NOT_REVIEWED`.
2. `NOT_APPOINTED` requires `reviewer: null`.
3. `APPOINTED` requires a complete reviewer object.
4. `NOT_STARTED` requires no evidence, findings, review date, or decision.
5. A completed review requires an appointed reviewer, completed execution, complete evidence, recorded findings or explicit zero-findings attestation, review date, and non-empty evidence references.
6. A granted decision requires a completed review.
7. `APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS` requires conditions and a re-review or expiration trigger.
8. `CHANGES_REQUIRED` requires an open approval-relevant finding, required remediation, and required re-review.
9. `BLOCKED` requires a blocking rationale.
10. `APPROVED` cannot coexist with unresolved BLOCKER or HIGH findings.
11. No status may be inferred from filename, file presence, content length, or schema validity alone.
12. Supersession must reference an earlier record of the same review type and artifact, with no self-reference or cycle.
13. Audit event IDs are unique, timestamps ordered, and prior/next states consistent.
14. Canonical preparation records contain no invented reviewer, participant, evidence, result, or decision.
15. Identity, qualification, authenticity, consent, and authority remain human or external-system responsibilities.
